PART 1: TECHNICAL PROJECT REPORT

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

InterviewIQ is an enterprise-grade, adaptive AI interview preparation and assessment platform designed to bridge the gap between candidate preparation and real-world hiring standards. Unlike traditional preparation tools that rely on static, predetermined question banks and generic conversational bots, InterviewIQ simulates realistic technical, behavioral, and resume-based interviews with real-time adaptive questioning, multidimensional rubric scoring, lexical accuracy verification, and live coding sandboxes.

Problem Statement:

  • Static & Predictable Questioning: Candidates memorize answers to common questions without learning to adapt to real-world interviewer drill-downs.
  • Superficial Feedback: Existing bots evaluate based merely on response length, handing out inflated scores (e.g., 4/5) even for incorrect answers.
  • Disconnection from Real Resumes: Generic tools do not cross-examine candidates on their actual projects, architectures, and tech stacks listed on their resumes.

3. System Architecture & Lifecycle Flow

  1. Configuration: Candidate selects Target Role, Interview Type (Technical, HR, Behavioral, Resume-Based, Mixed), Difficulty, and Duration.
  2. Session Creation: Server generates a unique session ID and writes a new row in interview_sessions in SQLite.
  3. Live Interview Room: Question 1 is presented with an active countdown timer. Candidate answers via text editor or voice mode.
  4. Lexical & Semantic Rubric Evaluation:
    • Gibberish Filter: Random keystroke smashing (e.g. lk,;l,;l,;l) is strictly scored 0.0 / 10.0.
    • Misconception Checker: Factually wrong claims are explicitly flagged in an alerts banner.
    • 10-Point Scoring: Evaluates Correctness, Relevance, Depth, Clarity, and Problem-Solving.
  5. Dynamic Adaptive Follow-up:
    • Score ≥ 7.5: Escalates to advanced architectural edge cases.
    • Score 4.5 – 7.4: Probes missing trade-offs and nuances.
    • Score < 4.5: Pivots to core fundamentals to re-calibrate.
